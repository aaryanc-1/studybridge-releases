    When you’re stuck

    Hints first.
    The thinking stays yours.

    • Hints before answers. Every question has hints you open one at a time, then a worked solution, so you get there yourself.
    • No AI chat. Students never chat with AI. Lessons, hints and solutions are written ahead of time.
    • Checked before you see it. Everything is checked before it reaches you, and anything can be reported in one tap.
    • With a tutor, the tutor decides. Your tutor approves everything before you see it.
    Q4 Solve 3x − 7 = 11
    Hint 1What could you do to both sides to get 3x on its own?
    Hint 2Adding 7 gives 3x = 18. What’s the last step?
    Worked solution3x − 7 = 11, so 3x = 18, so x = 6
    Open one at a time, only when you need it.

    Questions from students

    Can I use StudyBridge without a tutor?

    Yes. Sign up as a student and you get a plan to your exam, lessons, practice papers and mocks, with hints and worked solutions. Your first 7 days are free.

    Is there an AI chatbot?

    No. Students never chat with AI. Lessons, hints and worked solutions are written ahead of time and checked before you see them, so the thinking is always yours.

    Are the practice papers real past papers?

    No. They are original papers written to match each real paper’s format: sections, timing, marks and style. Real past papers open from the exam board’s own website. StudyBridge is not affiliated with or endorsed by Cambridge, Pearson Edexcel or the IB.

    My subject or exam isn’t listed.

    Ask for it on the subjects page. The most-requested come next. You’ll also be able to upload your own syllabus or course outline, from grade 7 to university, and get a plan from it.

    How old do I need to be?

    From grade 8 you can start on your own. Below grade 8, a parent sets up or approves the account. In some countries the law asks a parent to confirm for anyone under 18, and the app handles that for you.

    How much does it cost?

    $11 a month, or an exam pass at $9 a month paid once, lasting until your exams. Every subject at your level is included, and you can try it free for 7 days. See student pricing.
