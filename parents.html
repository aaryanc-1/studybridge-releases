What you see, and what you don’t

You see

  • A weekly report, approved by their tutor: lessons, marks, time studied and what’s next.
  • Upcoming lessons and due dates, so nothing is a surprise.
  • Marks once they’re given back, with strong and weak topics.
  • Mock grades and the exam countdown.

You never see

  • Their messages with their tutor
  • Their working, photos or drafts
  • Anything before their tutor has approved it
  • The exam camera

Your child can see which parents are linked to their account.

Questions from parents

Does it cost anything?

No. Parent accounts are free on every plan.

Does my child use AI?

No. With a tutor, Prof works only for the tutor, who approves everything first. On their own, your child uses lessons, hints and worked solutions written ahead and checked before they see them. There’s no AI chat.

Can I pay for my child?

Yes. You can pay for your child from your own free parent account: $11 a month, or an exam pass at $9 a month paid once. See pricing.

Can I message the tutor here?

No. Keep talking to the tutor the way you already do.

How do I get the weekly report?

In your parent account. If your child has switched it on, the tutor can also send it by WhatsApp or email.

What about my child’s data?

We collect only what’s needed for learning, never sell it or show ads, and you can ask the tutor, or us, to see or delete it. See the privacy policy.
